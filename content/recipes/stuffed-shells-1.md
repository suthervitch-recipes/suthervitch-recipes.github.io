ingredients:
  - 'FRESH RICOTTA:'
  - ∙ 2000g (8c) whole milk
  - ∙ 200g (1c) heavy cream
  - ∙ 10g (2tsp) salt
  - ∙ 100g (7T) white distilled vinegar
  - 'SHELLS:'
  - ∙ 340g (12oz) jumbo pasta shells
  - ∙ 160g (5.5oz) sliced prosciutto
  - ∙ 30g 6 cloves  minced garlic (15g for spinach, 15g for marinara)
  - ∙ 200g fresh spinach
  - ∙ 450g (1lb) ricotta cheese (recipe below)
  - ∙ 100g (1/2c) heavy cream
  - ∙ 75g (3/4c) shredded mozzarella
  - ∙ 75g (3/4c) grated parmesan, plus extra for topping
  - ∙ 1 large egg
  - ∙ 20 cranks of fresh black pepper
notes: |-
  From: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OdgOchwSifk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OdgOchwSifk)
  Originally used 15g salt, I went down to 10g.
  Used Serious Eats Best Italian American Tomato Sauce. 8 28oz cans more than enough for the recipe.
tags: []

Add milk + cream to saucier and heat with a thermometer to 190F.
Take it off the heat and add the vinegar, stir quickly then let it sit for 20m then strain through cheesecloth or paper towel in a strainer.

Chop up prosciutto
Fry prosciutto for a minute until soft but not crispy. Maybe a touch of olive oil
Add in Garlic and fry for 30s
Add in spinach and let it wilt. Keep flipping/stirring
Put it on cutting board and chop it up finely

Mix together everything for the shells

Boil the shell in water with a bit less salt than usual and under cook it. Maybe 7mins.
Pour out into a colander and immediately toss with olive oil to prevent sticking.

Stuff the shells and put them in a tray with tomato sauce nearly covering the shells

Cook in oven at 350f (maybe 375f?) for 40mins covered then 10mins uncovered.
If frozen, cook for 70mins covered then 10mins uncovered.
