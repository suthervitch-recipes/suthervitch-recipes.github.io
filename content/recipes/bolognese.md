ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  - 8 tablespoons (4 oz) unsalted butter, divided
  - 1 cup chopped onion
  - 1 1/3 cups chopped celery
  - 1 1/3 cups chopped carrot
  - 1 pound ground chuck, (I used 1/2 pound chuck and 1/2 pound veal)
  - 1/2 pound ground pork
  - kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 2 cups whole milk
  - 1/8 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg, or a pinch of ground nutmeg
  - 2 cups dry white wine
  - 3 cups canned imported San Marzano tomatoes, (Italian plum tomatoes) with their juice
  - 1 pound tagliatelle, (homemade or storebought), cooked and drained
  - freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, at the table
  - Bit of pancetta, maybe 100g
  - Spoonful of chopped calabrian chilis or red chili flakes
  - Teaspoon of better than bouillon
  - Optional Basil Stem
  - Optional Italian seasoning

Brown meat, put into bowl

Render pancetta, remove

Add onions, celery, carrot and maybe a teaspoon of salt and sweat for a few minutes

Add tomato paste and cook for a few minutes

Add Calabrian chilis and garlic and cook for a few minutes

Add dried italian seasonings if using

Add milk and reduce until almost dry

Add white wine and reduce until dry

Add tomatoes.

Simmer for few hours, adding water as needed

Add Basil stem with an hour or 2 left

Add cream and butter with 30 mins left
