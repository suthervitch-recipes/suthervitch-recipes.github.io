title: Gochujang Caramel Cookies
date: ''
description: A chewy sugar cookie gets a spicy-sweet twist from ribbons of gochujang caramel swirled through the dough. A touch of cinnamon adds warm, snickerdoodle-like notes, while the fermented chile paste balances the sweetness with heat. Mixing the dough by hand is recommended for the best texture and the most defined swirls.
stars: ''
portions: About 8 large cookies
prepTime: ''
cookTime: 45 mins (total time)

ingredients:
  - 115g (1/2 cup / 8 tbsp) unsalted butter, very soft, divided
  - 27g (2 packed tbsp) dark brown sugar
  - 20g (1 heaping tbsp) gochujang
  - 200g (1 cup) granulated sugar
  - 1 large egg, at room temperature
  - 3g (1/2 tsp coarse kosher salt, or 3/4 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt)
  - 0.5g (1/4 tsp) ground cinnamon
  - 5ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
  - 2.5g (1/2 tsp) baking soda
  - 185g (1 1/2 cups) all-purpose flour

1. Stir together 1 tablespoon (15g) of the butter, the brown sugar, and the gochujang in a small bowl until smooth. Set aside at room temperature.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the remaining butter, granulated sugar, egg, salt, cinnamon, and vanilla by hand until smooth, about 1 minute. Switch to a flexible spatula and stir in the baking soda. Add the flour and gently stir to combine. Refrigerate the dough until less sticky but still soft and pliable, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. While the dough chills, heat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line 2 large sheet pans with parchment.
4. Remove the dough from the fridge. Spoon the gochujang mixture over the dough in 3 to 4 separate blobs. Swirl it in with long circular strokes so you get distinct streaks of orange-red through the beige dough — don't overmix.
5. Scoop 1/4-cup rounds of dough, spacing them at least 3 inches apart, 4 to 5 per pan.
6. Bake until lightly golden at the edges and dry and set in the center, 11 to 13 minutes, rotating the pans halfway through.
7. Let cool completely on the sheet pan — the cookies will flatten slightly and continue cooking as they cool.
